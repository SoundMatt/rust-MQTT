### §7.4.4.4 — Cyclomatic complexity

Per IEC 61508-3 Annex A, V(G) ≤ 10 for SIL 2.

`rsfusa comp` is run in CI. Functions with V(G) > 10 fail the CI `comp` step.
Current highest known V(G): `handle_client` (broker, test-only, QM-rated) — excluded from SIL 2 scope.
